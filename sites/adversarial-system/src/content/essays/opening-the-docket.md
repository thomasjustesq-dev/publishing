The Adversarial System is a publication of essays on what happens where machine systems, state power, and individual rights collide.

This site is the designed, permanent home of each piece. Substack is the distribution channel — email and conversation — not the canonical text. When both exist, **this docket is authoritative**.

Expect filings on artificial intelligence, national security, civil liberties, and the institutions that mediate them. Argue in the open. Separate fact from judgment. Correct promptly when we get it wrong.

Subscribe on [Substack](https://theadversarialsystem.substack.com) for the next filing. Standards for how we treat claims live on the [Standards](/standards/) page.
